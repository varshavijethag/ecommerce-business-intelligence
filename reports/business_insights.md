# E-Commerce Business Insights

## Project Overview

This report summarizes the key business insights identified from the Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist.

## Key Insights

### 1. Sales Performance
- Best sales month: November 2017
- Sales during the best month: 1,010,271.37
- Sales growth from 2017 to 2018: 19.99%

### 2. Product Performance
- Top-selling category: Beauty & Health
- Category sales: 1,258,681.34
- Top-selling product ID: bb50f2e236e5eea0100680137654686c
- Product sales: 63,885.00

### 3. Customer Insights
- Top customer city: São Paulo
- Customers in São Paulo: 15,540
- Top customer state: SP
- Customers in SP: 41,746

### 4. Payment Insights
- Most-used payment method: Credit Card
- Credit card payment count: 76,795

### 5. Customer Satisfaction
- Average review score: 4.09 / 5

### 6. Delivery Performance
- Average delivery time: 12.09 days
- On-time delivery rate: 89.15%

### 7. Order Performance
- Delivered order rate: 97.02%

## Business Recommendations

1. Focus marketing efforts on high-performing product categories.
2. Maintain strong credit-card payment support.
3. Improve delivery performance to increase the on-time delivery rate.
4. Monitor customer reviews to identify areas for service improvement.
5. Analyze the São Paulo and SP markets for further growth opportunities.

## Dataset Note

The Olist dataset contains historical e-commerce data from 2016 to 2018. Some months contain partial-period data, so unusually low sales in those periods should not be interpreted as normal business performance.
